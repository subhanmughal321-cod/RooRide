🚗 RooRide — City Carpooling System
Share a ride. Save money. Travel smarter.

RooRide is a web-based carpooling platform designed to connect passengers and drivers travelling along similar routes in Gujranwala, Pakistan. It aims to make everyday travel more affordable, convenient and environmentally friendly, especially for university students and daily commuters.

Explore RooRide
✨ Features
Rider
- Search and filter rides by route, date and available seats.
- View ride details, fares and driver information.
- Demonstrate booking, cancellation and ride management.
- Explore saved routes, travel preferences, ratings and eco rewards.
Driver
- Register with driving licence, vehicle and verification details.
- Publish ride offers with routes, departure times, seats and fares.
- Accept or reject sample passenger booking requests.
- View ride history, vehicle information and sample performance statistics.
Administrator
- Manage sample user, driver and vehicle records.
- Demonstrate verification, ride and booking management.
- Review complaints, community requests and sample reports.

📌 Project Status
RooRide is currently an academic frontend prototype with a homepage, account selection, login and registration interfaces, and rider, driver and administrator dashboards. Several workflows use fictional JavaScript records.

Next steps include relational database design, PHP/MySQL integration, secure authentication, shared booking records, server-side verification and system testing. Real payments, GPS tracking, document storage and production security are not implemented. Demo records may reset on refresh. Please do not enter real personal information or identity documents.
